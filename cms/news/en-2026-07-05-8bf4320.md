The **2.8 New: Eridan Sunset** soundtrack album is now available with 20 tracks from the latest season finale, including Bootopia event themes, Phaethon boss themes, and Starlight Billy's EP tracks.

We've also added dedicated character albums for **Remielle**, **Promeia**, and **Starlight Billy**, so you can browse all their music in one place.

The **3.0 A Sleepwalker's Confession** album brings Season 3 to the library with 39 tracks exploring Roscaelifer, from district exploration themes to Scamall Hollow, CCD, and the Norma event soundtrack.

A new **Norma** character album is included, along with album covers for both version updates.

Open the news panel anytime to catch up on what's new — happy listening!
