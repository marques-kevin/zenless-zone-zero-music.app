We've added a comprehensive collection of new tracks from version 2.5 to the music library.

This update includes area themes and exploration music from locations like Lumite Mine, Old Port Factory, and Porcelume Processing Base. You'll also find battle themes for these areas, along with boss themes like the Lockspring Boss Theme.

Character-focused tracks have been added as well, including themes for Ye Shunguang and Phaethon, along with story moments like "Phaethon Saving Ye Shunguang Theme - The Hand in the Dark" and "Phaethon's Eyes Power Awakening Theme - First Signs of Change."

We've also included some additional tracks from versions 1.7 and 2.3, expanding the soundtrack collection even further.

You can now explore all these new tracks in the 2.5 album, bringing more of the game's immersive music to your listening experience.
