We've introduced a new ladder system that lets you rank your favorite characters and see how popular they are across the community.

You can now create your own character ranking and submit it to the ladder. The system aggregates all user rankings to show character popularity statistics, including which characters are most loved by the community.

The ladder page displays comprehensive statistics about character rankings, making it easy to see which characters are trending and how your favorites compare to others.
