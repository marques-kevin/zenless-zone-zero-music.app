We introduced an easier way to bring new music into the app.

A new tool now lets us automatically turn selected YouTube videos into audio tracks and add them directly to the library. We used it to bring in the latest 2.4 songs.

For you, this simply means that new music can appear in the app faster and more regularly, without waiting for a slow manual process.
