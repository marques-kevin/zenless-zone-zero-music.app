description: "Introduced an in-app news panel that explains recent updates in plain language."
language: en

We’ve added a new news panel inside the app so you can quickly see what has changed without needing to read technical release notes.

News items are now written in simple, everyday language and grouped by date, with a short explanation of what each change means for you. If you’re curious, you can still open the related change on GitHub using the small icon next to each date.

This should make it much easier to stay up to date with new songs, features, and behind‑the‑scenes improvements.
