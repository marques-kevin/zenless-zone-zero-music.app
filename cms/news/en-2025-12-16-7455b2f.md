We’ve added a big batch of new tracks from version 2.1. This includes:

- Area and event music from Waifei Peninsula, the Midsummer Fantasy / Fantasy Resort events, and the new Endless Tower: Glory mode.
- Combat and boss themes like "Zenith's Lingering Light" and the Miasmic Fiend boss track.
- Yuzuha’s EP "Wonderland Trickery" (both lyrics MV and instrumental versions), available in her character playlist and the 2.1 album.

If you enjoy exploring Waifei Peninsula or playing the new events, you can now listen to their full music selection directly in the app playlists.
