We've added several new tracks to the music library, bringing more of the Zenless Zone Zero soundtrack to your collection.

The new additions include Rina's Agent Story Combat Theme, a powerful track from the character's story. We've also added Ye Shunguang's Relaxing Theme, perfect for those peaceful moments. From version 2.5, we've included the Black Rabbit S'n-Z x HOYO-MiX collaboration track, showcasing the unique musical style of this collaboration.

We've also added Shining from version 1.5, expanding the collection of tracks from earlier game versions.

Along with these new tracks, we've introduced dedicated character albums for Rina, Ye Shunguang, and Astra Yao. These albums make it easier to find and enjoy all the music associated with your favorite characters, bringing their unique soundtracks together in one place.

You can now explore these new tracks and character albums in the music library, discovering the diverse sounds that make up the Zenless Zone Zero musical experience.
