We've added a new radio button feature that makes it easier to discover and enjoy random songs from the music library.

With this new feature, you can now easily switch to random playback mode, letting the app select songs for you automatically. This is perfect for when you want to discover new tracks or just let the music play without having to choose what to listen to next.

The radio button is now available in the player interface, giving you quick access to random song playback whenever you want to mix things up.
