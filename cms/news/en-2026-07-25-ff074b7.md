You can now suggest music to add to the app directly from the navbar.

Click the music request icon in the top bar, paste one or more YouTube links (one per line), and send your request. Each link is saved separately, so you can see which songs are still pending, which have been added, and which were declined.

If someone else already requested the same track, your request is grouped with theirs — so we avoid duplicates and you still get notified when it's added.

Log in with Google to submit and track your requests. Happy listening!
