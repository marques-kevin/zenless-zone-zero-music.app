The **Dreamland Fest** album is now in the library — 23 live performances from the HoYoFair **Random Play 2nd Anniversary** fantasy electronic music festival.

You'll find fan covers and remixes of ZZZ favorites like **Tiny Giant**, **ReDreaming Angel**, **404: Find Me**, **Rise Up**, **Marchenstunde**, and more, all in one playlist.

Look for **Dreamland Fest** in the jukebox section and enjoy the full concert experience.

Happy listening!
